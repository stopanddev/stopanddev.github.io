My Debt Finance

An internal budgeting tool for personal use.

About

My Debt Finance is a private, self-hosted budgeting application. It runs on a private network and is not available to the public. There is no sign-up, and it is not offered as a service to anyone else.

How bank data is used

The app uses Plaid to connect to the owner's own bank accounts. It reads account balances and transactions to:

  • Track spending and categorize transactions
  • Monitor debt balances and payoff progress
  • Show upcoming bills and due dates

Privacy and security

  • Data is used only by the account owner for budgeting.
  • Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • The database is encrypted at rest, and the app is reachable only over an encrypted private network.
  • Connections to Plaid use TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Bank connections can be removed at any time through Plaid Portal.
